Small business

AI adoption for owners and teams.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Everything is explained in plain language, and the written plan and policy are meant to be read by anyone on your team.

How big does my business need to be?

There’s no minimum. The packages work well for teams of roughly 2 to 100 people. If you’re larger, we can scope something that fits.

Where do I start?

With a free 30-minute call, or on your own with the AI readiness calculator and the AI safe-use checklist. If it’s a fit, the next step is the readiness review.

What does the free AI readiness calculator tell me?

In about five minutes it estimates the hours AI could give back to your business each month, priced at Austin-area wages, gives you a readiness score across tools, policy, security and people, lists the rules that apply to your industry, and suggests three workflows to start with. No sign-up needed. Try the calculator.

Is it safe to put customer information into AI tools?

Sometimes, with the right tool and settings, and sometimes not at all. Sorting that out is a core part of the readiness review and the safe-use policy.

Do you work with Microsoft Copilot or Google Gemini?

Yes. Both can search the files a person has access to, so the most important step happens before you turn them on: making sure sensitive files aren’t shared more widely than they should be. I help with that, then with setup and training.

Will you sell me software?

No. I don’t resell AI tools or take commissions, so recommendations are based on what fits your business. Often that means making better use of tools you already pay for.

How much does it cost?

Each package has a fixed price, agreed before any work starts, and the ongoing support plan is month to month. The readiness review is the smallest commitment and tells you whether the rest is worth doing.

Tech companies

Competitive evaluations, technical marketing and enablement.

How does an engagement start?

With a short call about what you need to learn and why (book one here). If it’s a fit, I send a one-page proposal with scope, deliverables, timeline and a fixed fee. We sign an NDA before any detailed scoping.

Whose licenses and environments do you test with?

Only ones you’re authorized to provide, or that we set up together under the vendors’ terms. Lab licenses and cloud costs are provided by or billed to the client, so you own the environment and the evidence.

How do you handle confidentiality?

Findings, test data and lab access stay under NDA and are shared only with the people you name. At the end, the lab is handed off to your team or cleaned up, whichever you prefer.

What do you need from my team?

A point of contact, access to the products and accounts in scope, and about an hour a week for status updates and decisions. I handle the day-to-day lab work.

Do you work with buyers, not just vendors?

Yes. MSSPs, resellers and other buyers hire me to test a shortlist of products against their real needs before they commit, and to recommend the one that fits.

How is pricing set?

Evaluations and documentation sprints are fixed-fee projects. Fractional product marketing and lab operations are monthly retainers. Content and enablement assets can be priced per piece. You’ll know the full cost before we start.

Working with Ares

Who, where and how.

Who will I work with?

Directly with Fernando Jorge, the founder. Ares Technical has been an independent consulting practice since 2017 and is organized as Ares Technical LLC. There’s no handoff to junior staff. More about Fernando.

Do you work in person or remotely?

Both. For small businesses, I visit and run on-site training across Williamson County, including Round Rock, Georgetown, Cedar Park, Leander, Hutto, Taylor and Liberty Hill. In Puerto Rico, Henry Moczo, our team member on the island, works with clients in person, and we meet by video anywhere on the island. Technology companies and clients anywhere else work with me remotely.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. For technology companies, an NDA is signed before any detailed scoping. Small businesses can ask for one too, and customer data you share during a review is handled confidentially.

How do I get started?

Book a free 30-minute call, or send a message from the small business or tech company contact form. I reply within two business days.
