What I write

Whatever the format, my approach is the same: lead with the reader’s real problem, give them a structure they can act on, and back every claim with evidence.

    Data reports, ebooks, whitepapers and executive playbooks.

  • Product marketing

    Solution briefs, datasheets, feature briefs and infographics.

  • Customer and partner stories

  • Technical documentation

  • Cyberhaven2026
    Co-authored whitepaper

    Inside a CISO’s Playbook: What Agentic Security Looks Like in Practice

    How one security team uses AI agents to keep pace with engineering, told for CISOs and the engineers who report to them.

    Read the case study

    The challenge

    Turn three internal AI-agent projects from Cyberhaven’s security team into a credible story about scaling security with AI, without it reading like a sales pitch.

    My approach

    • I opened with a concrete tension, a 200:1 ratio of engineers to application security staff, so readers feel the problem before any solution appears.
    • I gave every agent the same three-line summary (what it is, what it does, what it means), then a technical deep dive and an architecture diagram. Executives can stop at the summary; engineers can keep going.
    • I credited each engineering lead by name, so the paper reads as peer-to-peer practitioner content.
    • I closed with results and open questions to peers, inviting a conversation rather than a hard sell.

  • Cyberhaven2026
    Research report

    2026 AI Adoption and Risk Report: Six-Month Update

    Six months of enterprise telemetry, turned into one clear finding about where AI risk is moving.

    Read the case study

    The challenge

    Turn product telemetry into one clear story for security leaders and press, while staying honest about what the data does and doesn’t prove.

    My approach

    • I put the key numbers up front so skimmers and journalists find the quotable findings in seconds.
    • I built a single thesis from two datasets: chat-based AI tools are consolidating while endpoint AI agents are accelerating, so risk is moving from applications to workflows.
    • I flagged the limits of the data, separating share of use from overall adoption and labeling one explanation a working hypothesis.
    • I ended by translating the findings into what security programs should do next.

  • Forcepoint2019
    Ebook

    Rethinking Data Security with a Risk-adaptive Approach

    One employee’s day, told twice, to show why block-or-allow data protection falls short.

    Read the case study

    The challenge

    Make the case for a new category, risk-adaptive protection, to buyers used to the block-or-allow model.

    My approach

    • I built the ebook around one persona, Kate the research chemist, and told her story twice: once under traditional rules, where a harmless USB backup is blocked, and once under risk-adaptive protection, where low-risk Kate works freely and phished Kate is contained.

Engineering documentation

Before moving into cybersecurity content, I wrote documentation as an engineer. Much of it was internal or customer-confidential, so I describe it here. Samples are available on request with sensitive details removed.

  • Product manuals and installation guides

    At Edgewater Networks I contributed to the EdgeView Virtual Appliance installation and user guides and the EdgeMarc VoIP System user guide, covering VMware deployment, licensing, system sizing and administration.

  • QA test plans

    I authored structured test plans for dual-WAN and LTE failover and for FXS/FXO telephony features, with defined terms, numbered test steps and expected results for every scenario.

  • Interoperability and configuration guides

    I wrote a step-by-step BroadWorks provisioning guide that pairs each CLI command with its expected output, plus quick references for enabling network services.

  • Provisioning guidelines and engineering memos

    On AT&T’s switch design team, I documented automated IP phone provisioning, including architecture, templates and troubleshooting built from lab-tested failure scenarios, plus concise configuration memos.
