Most small businesses I talk to are in the same spot.

  • “Everyone says we should use AI.”

    But nobody has said where it fits in your business, or what it should replace.

How I help

It starts with a free call. If it’s a fit, the readiness review comes next, and you add the rest only if you need it. Each package has a fixed price agreed before we start, with no long contracts; the support plan is month to month.

  • 01Start here

    AI readiness review

    A short, fixed-price look at how your business runs today, where AI can save real time, and where it could put customer or company data at risk.

    • Conversations with you and key staff
    • A list of tasks ranked by time saved and risk
    • A short written plan with clear next steps
  • 02

    Tool selection and setup

    Help choosing the right AI tools for your needs and budget, often starting with what you already pay for, then setting them up with sensible privacy settings.

    • A side-by-side shortlist in plain language
    • Accounts and settings configured
    • A simple pilot on one or two real tasks
  • 03

    Safe-use policy

    A one-to-two page set of rules your team can actually follow: which tools are approved, what information never goes in, and when a person must check the output.

    • A written AI use policy
    • A quick-reference sheet for staff
    • Guidance for handling customer data
  • 04

    Team training

    Hands-on sessions built around your own work, so people learn to write good prompts, check results and know when not to use AI.

    • On-site in Williamson County and north Austin, or live online
    • Examples from your own tasks
    • Follow-up questions answered
  • 05

    Copilot and Gemini readiness

    AI assistants like Microsoft Copilot and Google Gemini can search everything a person has access to. Before you switch them on, I check who can see which files, so a payroll sheet or client contract shared too widely doesn’t show up in someone’s AI answer.

    • A review of file sharing and permissions
    • Fixes for overshared folders and links
    • A safe rollout plan for your team
  • 06

    Ongoing AI support plan

    AI tools change every month. A light monthly plan keeps your setup safe and useful without hiring anyone.

    • A monthly check-in call
    • New tools reviewed before your team uses them
    • Policy and settings updated as tools change

Questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. Everything is explained in plain language, and the written plan and policy are meant to be read by anyone on your team.

Is it safe to put customer information into AI tools?

Sometimes, with the right tool and settings, and sometimes not at all. Sorting that out is a core part of the readiness review and the safe-use policy.

How much does it cost?

Each package has a fixed price, agreed before any work starts. The readiness review is the smallest commitment and tells you whether the rest is worth doing.
