About

I’ve spent more than twenty years between the lab and the field: testing vendor products and integrations at AT&T, running a technical marketing lab at Edgewater Networks, then leading hands-on competitive work at Forcepoint, Devo, Splunk and Cyberhaven.

The pattern was always the same. Teams needed to know how products really behave, and they needed that evidence turned into something people could use. Ares Technical offers both, as a single owner, without adding headcount.

Today I also bring that security background to small businesses: helping owners pick the right AI tools, set simple rules and train their teams without putting customer data at risk.

    Interactive research tool

    DSPM Recs: evidence before rankings

    A DSPM-only comparison prototype that makes source coverage, review relevance, and missing evidence visible instead of presenting unsupported scores.

    Read the case study

    The challenge

    Vendor-wide reviews often mix DSPM with DLP and other security products. A useful comparison needs product-specific evidence and clear source boundaries.

    The approach

    • Created a six-vendor comparison interface with separate planned coverage for Reddit, G2, and Gartner Peer Insights.
    • Built a private JSON import and review workflow with DSPM relevance decisions, permission records, duplicate handling, and sanitized public exports.
    • Kept Reddit user sentiment separate from review-platform star ratings and removed historical DLP scores from the public prototype.

    Current status

    The public prototype and private review workflow are built. Live data collection and source licensing are not connected; the demo explicitly shows where evidence is missing.

    Inside a CISO’s Playbook: What Agentic Security Looks Like in Practice

    How one security team uses AI agents to keep pace with engineering, told for CISOs and the engineers who report to them.

    Read the case study

    The challenge

    Turn three internal AI-agent projects from Cyberhaven’s security team into a credible story about scaling security with AI, without it reading like a sales pitch.

    My approach

    • I opened with a concrete tension, a 200:1 ratio of engineers to application security staff, so readers feel the problem before any solution appears.
