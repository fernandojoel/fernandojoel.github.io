      How these numbers are calculated

      Hours. For each task: weekly team hours × the share AI can realistically take on (a low and high estimate) × 4.33 weeks. The shares are cautious Ares Technical estimates, from 10% to 45% depending on the task. They aren't the 50–75% many calculators assume. The low end is the main figure.

      Value. Each hour is priced at the Austin-area mean wage for the people who usually do that task (BLS, May 2025): office and admin , marketing and content $35.56, owner and manager time $71.09, and your industry's professional rate . Wages don't include benefits or payroll tax, so true cost is higher.

      Sanity check. In a St. Louis Fed survey, workers who used generative AI saved about 5.4% of their work hours on average. If your estimate is far above that, it assumes a well set-up team using AI daily.

      Readiness. Four areas worth 25 points each: Tools (are people on business accounts), Policy (written rules and client-data handling), Security (two-step login and a written data-protection plan), People (training). In each area, 18 or more points is Observed, 9–17 is Partial and under 9 is a Gap.
