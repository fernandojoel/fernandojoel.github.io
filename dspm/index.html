02 / HOW EVIDENCE EARNS ITS PLACE

DSPM is the filter, not just the label.

01

Identify the actual product

A vendor name is not enough. Confirm the DSPM product or module and the relevant use case.

02

Review the context

Include DSPM discovery, classification, exposure, access risk, and posture remediation. Exclude unrelated DLP, endpoint, and generic vendor commentary.

03

Verify permission

Collection, analysis, public summaries, links, and metrics need an applicable source agreement. Imported records remain private until the publication checks pass.

04

Keep sources distinct

Reddit user sentiment and review-platform star ratings measure different things. Display them separately, with counts and dates, never as one blended score.

Ranking and limitations

Reddit sentiment groups evidence by a pseudonymous author key within each vendor. Positive without negative is positive; negative without positive is negative; otherwise neutral. Missing author keys are excluded from user-based scores. The confidence measure is the 95% Wilson lower bound, including neutral users; no score is shown below five users. G2 and Gartner averages use only the eligible imported ratings, not the platforms’ overall product ratings. Cross-platform identities are not merged.

DSPM relevance is a human review decision. Keyword matching suggests records for review; it does not establish product quality or relevance. There is no claim that the shortlist represents the complete current DSPM market.
